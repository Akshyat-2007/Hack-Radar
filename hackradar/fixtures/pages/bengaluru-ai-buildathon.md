# Bengaluru AI Buildathon 2026

**Organizer**: TechSparks Bengaluru  
**Dates**: November 5 - 6, 2026  
**Registration Closes**: November 2, 2026  
**Location**: Koramangala Indoor Stadium, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India  
**Mode**: In-person  
**Prize Pool**: ₹5,00,000 INR  

## About The Hackathon
Join 500+ builders, engineers, and researchers for 48 hours of intense hacking focused on Autonomous AI Agents, Large Language Model optimization, and Generative AI applications.

## Eligibility & Rules
- Open to all developers, founders, researchers, and university students across India.
- Teams of 1 to 4 participants.
- In-person attendance in Bengaluru is mandatory for the final presentation.
- Mentors and GPU compute credits provided by cloud partners.

## Tracks
1. Autonomous Agents & Tool Use
2. Edge AI & On-device Inference
3. Enterprise Knowledge Retrieval (RAG)
4. Creative Generative Media
